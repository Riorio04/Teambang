# Style brief (from 4 reference samples, guide only, not to copy)

## What the samples share
- ~22–23 s of real content (the rest is the TikTok end card, which we don't make)
- Hard cuts timed to the music beat, no fancy transitions
- Cinematic teal-and-orange color grade: warm highlights, cool shadows, rich contrast
- One title moment early (0–8 s), then pure footage
- Everyday city "moments": transport, streets, coffee, walking, golden hour

## Pacing patterns observed
| Ref | Format | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| A (city commute) | 16:9 | Steady cut every ~1.35 s, no text |
| B (travel) | 16:9 | Photo-grid intro + big title, then rapid cuts every ~0.5 s, floating photo-in-photo cards |
| C (daily recap) | 16:9 | Quick 0.3 s cuts in the intro, small kicker + bold condensed title + spaced subtitle, then cut every ~1.2 s |
| D (mini vlog) | 9:16 | Serif title ("Mini / VLOG / today's activities") held over every shot, cut every ~1.15 s |

## Our own house style (original, inspired by the above)
- Delivery: 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, ~15–30 s
- Grade: our own warm-cinematic LUT, adjusted per clip so everything matches
- Opening hook in the first 1 s (strongest shot), title card by about 2 s
- Our own title designs and wording (no "YOUR LOCATION" / "DAILY MOMENT" / "Mini VLOG" lookalikes)
- Cut length by mood: chill = 1.2–1.5 s, energetic = 0.4–0.6 s bursts
- Photos: gentle push-in/pan so they move like video
- Keep captions and text inside the TikTok/Reels safe zone (clear of the bottom ~20% and the right-side buttons)
